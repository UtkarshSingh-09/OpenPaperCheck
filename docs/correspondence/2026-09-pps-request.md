# Correspondence: Problematic Paper Screener (PPS) Inquiry

**Date:** 2026-09-29  
**From:** Maintainers of OpenPaperCheck (`contact@openpapercheck.org`)  
**To:** Maintainers of Problematic Paper Screener (Guillaume Cabanac et al.)  
**Subject:** OpenPaperCheck integration: tortured phrases dataset license & collaboration  

---

Dear Dr. Cabanac and the Problematic Paper Screener Team,

We are developing **OpenPaperCheck** (https://github.com/UtkarshSingh-09/OpenPaperCheck), an open-source tool and public dataset designed to help researchers verify post-publication notices, errata, and retracted citations before building on prior work.

We deeply admire the pioneering work done by PPS on identifying tortured phrases and automated scientific text anomalies.

Under our project roadmap (Milestone M4, Signals & Evidence Cards), we plan to surface detected tortured phrases exclusively as **candidate evidence** that requires volunteer confirmation—never as automated accusatory verdicts.

We would like to inquire about:
1. The licensing terms governing the redistribution or programmatic lookup of PPS's curated list of tortured phrases and question marks.
2. The preferred format and attribution text for citing PPS when displaying flagged phrase matches in our open-source evidence cards.
3. Opportunities to feed human-verified consensus verdicts back to PPS or open datasets.

Thank you for your tireless dedication to scientific integrity.
