# Correspondence: PubPeer Collaboration & Terms Inquiry

**Date:** 2026-09-29  
**From:** Maintainers of OpenPaperCheck (`contact@openpapercheck.org`)  
**To:** PubPeer Foundation (`contact@pubpeer.com`)  
**Subject:** OpenPaperCheck integration: inquiry regarding automated commentary links & API terms  

---

Dear PubPeer Foundation Team,

We are developing **OpenPaperCheck** (https://github.com/UtkarshSingh-09/OpenPaperCheck), an open-source, non-profit academic integrity tool that allows researchers to inspect published papers and their reference lists for retractions and verifiable post-publication notices.

Our project operates under strict ethical guidelines ([ETHICS.md](https://github.com/UtkarshSingh-09/OpenPaperCheck/blob/main/ETHICS.md)):
1. **Attribute, don't accuse:** We never accuse papers or authors; we report only sourced external facts from recognized scholarly bodies.
2. **Zero people features:** We never collect, profile, or rank authors, institutions, or countries.
3. **No bulk scraping:** We do not scrape platforms whose terms of service prohibit automated database replication.

In our current CLI and upcoming web interface, we provide an outbound link to PubPeer search (`https://pubpeer.com/search?q={doi}`) so readers can inspect community discussions directly on your platform.

We would like to inquire about:
1. Whether you provide an official API or approved mechanism for open-source non-profit tools to check comment counts (e.g. *"This paper has 4 comments on PubPeer"*).
2. Any specific terms, rate limits, or attribution requirements your foundation requests for open science utilities referencing PubPeer.

We look forward to collaborating in promoting transparency and rigorous scholarship.
